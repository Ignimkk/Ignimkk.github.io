## About

I am a robotics researcher working on **intelligent robot manipulation, perception, and motion planning**. My research focuses on making learned robotic actions more reliable by combining vision-language-action (VLA) policies with geometric scene understanding, collision reasoning, and trajectory optimization.

## Research Projects

### AG3S — Target Surface Grounding for Selective Contact

Ongoing research on grounding task-relevant 3D target surfaces and building geometry-aware collision constraints for robot manipulation. The goal is to enable contact with intended targets while avoiding unintended contacts with neighboring objects.

### Dynamics-Aware Trajectory Optimization

Research on generating robot manipulator trajectories under kinematic, dynamic, and collision constraints, with an emphasis on feasibility and smooth execution.
